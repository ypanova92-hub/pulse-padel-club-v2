Contact us →
Do I need prior experience to play padel?

No, padel is easy to pick up and perfect for all levels. We offer introductory sessions for beginners, as well as coaching for intermediate and advanced players.

How do I book a court?

Compare peak and off-peak court rates, choose 60 or 90 minutes, then pick your date and time on Playtomic.

What should I bring?

Comfortable sportswear, suitable court shoes and a water bottle. Need a racket? Premium rackets are available to rent at the club.

Do you offer training sessions?

Yes. Choose private coaching, group classes or beginner sessions. Tell us your level and goals and we’ll help you find the right fit.

Do I need a membership to play?

Book individual court sessions with no monthly package commitment. Compare peak and off-peak rates in Pricing, then choose your time on Playtomic.

Can I host a private event at PULSE?

Yes. From team padel nights to group sessions, contact us and we’ll help you plan the details.
